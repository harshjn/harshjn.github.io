# Hi, I'm Harsh 👋

I am a post-doc and joined as **Simons' Career Development Fellow** at the National Centre for Biological Sciences (NCBS), Bengaluru [1] in 2024. My research and projects sit at the intersection of experimental soft matter physics, engineering, and biophysics.

---

## 🔬 Soft Matter Physics
I specialize in analyzing non-linear material behaviors through advanced simulation frameworks and microscopy setups.

*   **Finite Element Methods (FEM):** Utilizing the Simulation Open Framework Architecture (**SOFA**) and **GMSH** to model hyper-elastic materials, focusing on parametric mesh generation and simulating self-friction [1].
    *   *Presentation:* Featured at **SOFA WEEK 2023**.
*   **Non-linear Buckling:** Developed a **MATLAB solver** using numerical techniques for the dynamic buckling behavior of thin soft sheets [1].
*   **Experimental Setups:** I have extensive experience building microscopy-based setups from scratch, including **SLM-based Optical Tweezers** and micropipette aspiration [1].

## ⚙️ Engineering Projects
I enjoy translating theoretical models into practical engineering solutions, from fluid dynamics to hardware prototyping.

*   **CFD Simulations:** Studied fluid-structure interactions, drag forces, and vortex generation on mechanical bodies [1]. Notably, I designed and simulated a space toilet concept as a freelance project [1].
*   **Nastran & 3D Printing:** Performed stress-strain optimization for cellular structures by varying 3D printing slicing parameters [1].
*   **Arduino & Hardware:** Designed and built a custom **Pen Plotter** from scratch using Arduino-based electronics [1].
