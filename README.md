# Hi, I'm Harsh 👋

I am a **Simons' Career Development Fellow** at the National Centre for Biological Sciences (NCBS), Bengaluru [1]. My research and projects sit at the intersection of experimental soft matter physics, computational engineering, and creative writing.

---

## 🔬 Physics & Research
I specialize in analyzing non-linear material behaviors through advanced simulation frameworks.

*   **Finite Element Methods (FEM):** Utilizing the Simulation Open Framework Architecture (**SOFA**) and **GMSH** to model hyper-elastic materials. My work focuses on parametric mesh generation and simulating self-friction and self-interaction [1].
    *   *Presentation:* Featured at **SOFA WEEK 2023**.
*   **Non-linear Buckling:** Developed a **MATLAB solver** using numerical techniques for the dynamic buckling behavior of thin soft sheets [1].

## ⚙️ Engineering Projects
I enjoy translating theoretical models into practical engineering solutions, from fluid dynamics to hardware prototyping.

*   **CFD Simulations:** Studied fluid-structure interactions, drag forces, and vortex generation on mechanical bodies [1]. Notably, I designed and simulated a space toilet concept as a freelance project [1].
*   **Nastran & 3D Printing:** Performed stress-strain optimization for cellular structures by varying 3D printing slicing parameters [1].
*   **Arduino & Hardware:** Designed and built a custom **Pen Plotter** from scratch using Arduino-based electronics [1].

## ✍️ Leadership & Writing
Beyond technical projects, I am passionate about community engagement and storytelling.

*   **Editorial Leadership:** Led the editing and publishing of *CRESCENDO*, the student magazine at TIFR during my graduate studies [1].
*   **Social Impact:** Spearheaded social welfare initiatives via NGOs during my undergraduate years, including the "Liter of light" project with **EWB-DTU**, winning recognition at Nirmaan – IIT Bombay [1].
*   **Creative Blog:** I actively maintain a blog dedicated to non-fiction and fiction storytelling [1].
